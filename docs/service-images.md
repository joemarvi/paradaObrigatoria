# Imagens dos Serviços

Imagens ilustrativas geradas com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, uma chamada por serviço. Não representam fotografias do estabelecimento. Os originais foram preservados; os arquivos usados no site estão em `public/services`.

Dark clean professional detailing bay, graphite black vehicle, realistic water and material texture, cool blue light with subtle warm yellow accents matching a black-and-yellow website. Center the actual service action clearly, with generous crop-safe margins. No text, no typography, no watermarks, no brand logos, no collage. A black sedan in three-quarter front view being washed with a pressure washer, abundant white soap foam on hood and windshield and a visible controlled water spray. Only a worker's gloved hand holding the spray lance enters from the side. Realistic professional car washing.
